## Top-line verdict

**The product surface (website, daily digests, weekly summaries) is healthy.** The data pipeline that feeds it is **partially broken in ways that the current alerting doesn't catch**. Specifically: the daily ingest cron has fired on schedule for 14 consecutive nights but **inserted zero articles on 11 of those 14 nights**; structured financial extraction (the `article_extractions` table) is running but **producing zero usable rows** (46/46 rows have `extraction_confidence=0` and `rows_with_data=0`); and the financial_ratios refresh path is **dead** (Yahoo 401, Cap IQ not configured). Articles are still landing daily because manual backfills are catching them, but you should not be the load-bearing component in your own data pipeline.

Maturity verdict: the May 11/12 audits called this a "functional MVP with good auditability, weak resilience." That's still true. The auditability got better (status page, pipeline_runs telemetry, freshness banner). The pipeline reliability got worse, because we added more failure modes that the alerts don't catch.

## What's still broken (ranked)

### P0 — silently broken, alerting doesn't catch

#### 1. Structured extraction is dead — `article_extractions` is 46 rows of zeros

```
total=46, positive_conf=0, avg_conf=0.000, rows_with_data=0
```

Every extraction row in the last 14 days has `extraction_confidence=0` and ZERO rows have a populated `revenue_figure`, `ebitda_figure`, or `guidance_verbatim`. The "track failure forward" change (always-write) made the table non-empty but masked the underlying problem: **Anthropic extraction calls are returning null/empty for 100% of articles**. The audit on 5/12 said "5 consecutive days with `positive_conf=0` should alert"; we're at 14 days and there's no alert.

This is the single biggest data-quality gap in the system. The extraction layer is what gives you structured financial signal (revenue, margins, guidance) that distinguishes a real intel product from a press-clipping service. Right now you have press-clippings + LLM-generated summaries, not extracted signal.

**Likely root causes** (in order of probability):
- The extraction prompt is being given headlines + brief excerpts, not full article bodies. Body-fetch succeeded for 45 articles but only 23 had >500 chars of body text. The prompt may be returning "insufficient data" when there's nothing to extract from.
- The JSON parsing in `lib/extraction.ts` may be rejecting valid responses (loose schema vs strict parsing).
- The model may be returning structured `null` correctly, and the issue is that 90% of articles legitimately don't contain hard financial figures — but then `extraction_confidence` should still be set to *something* on the "no figures present" verdict.

**Fix:** Sample 3 rows' `additional_metrics.failure_reason`; if they're all "no body text," the fix is upstream (body fetch). If they're "json parse error," the fix is in `lib/extraction.ts`. Add an alert: `5+ consecutive days of positive_conf=0 across all extractions` → email Gavin.

**11 of last 14 nights inserted zero articles.** Articles are landing in the database (last 7d: 23 articles, last 30d: 98) — but they're being added by *manual backfill runs*, not the cron. See May 11 email log: 6 separate digests sent the same morning, all from manual backfill batches (15, 32, 1, 1, 9, 12 articles).

The result is an email firehose: **9 zero-article alerts + 7 digests + 1 stale-queue alert = 17 emails in 14 days**. The signal-to-noise is poisoning the alert channel.

**Likely causes:** The cron at 04:00 UTC hits Google News feeds at a time when same-day publisher coverage is too sparse to clear the whitelist/dedup gates. By the time Gavin manually backfills mid-morning, fresh articles have hit the feeds and pass. Alternatively, the 8 RSS feed queries are too narrow.

**Fix options:**
- Shift cron to 11:00 UTC (post-market-open, more publisher activity)
- Widen `when:Nd` window from default to `when:4d` already shipped; bump to `when:7d`
- Suppress zero-article alert if a successful backfill happened the same day
- Add a "no articles for N days even after retries" escalation alert, suppress the per-day noise

#### 3. `pipeline_runs` telemetry table is mostly empty — only 2 rows in 14 days

The whole point of `pipeline_runs` is to track url_decode_pct, body_fetch_pct, anthropic_pct trends over time. We have 2 rows in 14 days — both from manual backfills on May 12. The nightly cron path is not writing to this table at all, which means the status page's "14d rates" graph is showing data from 2 manual runs, not the actual production pipeline.

**Fix:** Find where `pipeline_runs` insert is supposed to happen in `api/daily-scan.ts` — it's either guarded by a flag that's off in the cron path or the insert path errors silently. ~30 min investigation.

## Honest read

The system works at the "delivers something useful to the inbox most mornings" level. It does not yet work at the "I can show this to a $500K/quarter buyer with confidence" level. The gap between those two is the items in the P0 list. None of them are hard. They're just hidden because the alerting catches the wrong things.

The 14-day pattern in the data tells the story clearly: **you ship a feature, the pipeline degrades silently, you discover it days later, you ship a fix, repeat.** Adding observability that catches degradation *the day it happens* is the highest-leverage investment from here. The pipeline_runs telemetry table is the right idea; it just isn't being populated. Fix that one thing and the rest of the audit gets a lot easier.
