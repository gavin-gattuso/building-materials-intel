# H1 2026 M&A & Strategic-Deal Digest

For the report's **M&A callout box / "Consolidation Wave" section.** All items from the live KB (Jan–Jun 2026). The half's defining corporate story: a broad consolidation wave across distribution, cement/aggregates, and HVAC, capped by Berkshire's vote of confidence in homebuilding.

## ⭐ The headline: Berkshire's housing bet
- **Berkshire Hathaway → Taylor Morrison, ~$8.5B** (announced May 31, 2026). All-cash, **$72.50/share**, ~$6.8B equity / **$8.5B enterprise value**, a **24% premium** to the May 29 close. Taylor Morrison runs 350+ communities across 21 markets in 12 states (entry-level, move-up, resort).
  - *Why it matters (sales angle):* a famously conservative, long-horizon institution making a **direct, premium bet on U.S. homebuilding** at the bottom of a beleaguered cycle. CNBC framing: the deal "suggests the housing market may have bottomed." This is the report's signature "smart money sees a bottom" anchor.
  - Sources: *CNBC* (Jun 1), *ENR* (Jun 3).
