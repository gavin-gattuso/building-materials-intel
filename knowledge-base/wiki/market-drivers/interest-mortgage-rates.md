## Impact on Building Materials
Rates above 6% remain a binding constraint on existing-home turnover and entry-level new construction. The brief April dip toward 6.20% has not held — combined with the oil/tariff cost shock (see *Material & Energy Costs*), affordability is again tightening. Lower-rate scenarios (Fannie's sub-6% year-end) would unlock pent-up demand; the H2 trajectory depends largely on Fed reaction to inflation pass-through.
